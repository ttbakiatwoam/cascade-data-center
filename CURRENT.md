## November 16 audit change controls

- Corrected the **Major Industrial Facility** definition to **meeting or exceeding** the §165.11(D)(8) thresholds throughout current proposed materials.
- Changed independently proposed routine elevated-noise generator testing from **6 a.m.–8 p.m.** to **8 a.m.–6 p.m., Monday–Friday**, with emergency exceptions and controlling air/noise rules still applicable; this is a **proposed edit**, not a November 11 committee vote.
- Added proposed applicant disclosures for generator certification, Iowa DNR permit/exemption status, potential-to-emit review and operating records.
- **Unchanged:** the four reported committee-approved definition bodies, including the conjunctive Hyperscale definition, and the unapproved status of the independently proposed citywide prohibition.
- Resolved several misleading audit inferences. In particular, **combined NMHC+NOx** cannot automatically be called NOx for a Title V source threshold; Iowa's 500-hour PTE method is conditional; fuel sulfur can involve 15 ppm **or** the separate 1,000 ppm category depending on engine displacement.

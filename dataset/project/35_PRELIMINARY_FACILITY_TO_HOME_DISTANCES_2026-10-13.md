### Reproducible image measurement

1. The parcel image is **2,048 × 1,046 pixels**. The 400.50-ft line runs from about image x=**1,312** at the Simple Mining property boundary to x=**1,810** at the residential parcel boundary.
2. **400.5 ft ÷ (1,810 − 1,312) px = 0.8042 ft per aerial pixel.**
3. The closest visible house rooflines begin at roughly aerial x=**1,855–1,865**, so the site property line-to-roofline separation is **about 435–445 ft**. This is *not* the building-to-home measurement.
4. In the **1,371 × 904** concept screenshot, the main building spans roughly x=**384–766**, or **382 plan pixels**. The labeled **480-ft** length provides about **1.257 ft per plan pixel**.
5. The preliminary plan eastern parcel boundary lies near x=**949**. Approximate eastern edges of the cooling yard, main building, electrical yard and substation are x=**815, 766, 784 and 483**, respectively.
6. Eastward setbacks within the Simple Mining parcel are therefore about **170, 230, 205 and 585 ft**, respectively. Adding about **435 ft** from the site boundary to nearby visible rooflines gives **~605, 665, 640 and 1,020 ft**. These are rounded in the public results above. 
7. An overlay can be generated by aligning the east boundary of both screenshots and using **(480/382)/(400.5/498) ≈ 1.562 aerial pixels per concept pixel**. This is a **working illustration**, not survey-quality georeferencing.

The measured east-west differences are appropriate for the nearest homes because visible rooflines overlap the approximate north-south positions of these facilities. Other houses and actual sound sources could produce different closest point-to-point distances.

## What the figures do and do not prove

- They show the **preliminary main building is not on the residential property line**. The closest visible occupied homes appear hundreds of feet farther away than a simple parcel-line comparison implies.
- They do **not** establish what residents will hear. Sound depends on equipment type, fan speeds, multiple sources, low-frequency/tonal content, enclosures, barriers, topography, atmospheric conditions and existing industrial sound.
- The tree row is **conceptual landscaping**, not evidence of guaranteed acoustic shielding.
- The cooling yard could be acoustically more relevant than the main building. Acoustic modeling should use the **actual equipment positions**, not a single arbitrary building center.
- The neighboring open parcel can change with future development. Its current open condition is **not a deeded protection** for either party.
- Require the stamped final site plan, land survey and independently reviewed **day/night dBA/dBC and frequency-aware receptor model**. Confirm post-construction sound at both property lines and occupied homes. Use neutral standards for comparable M-2 uses.

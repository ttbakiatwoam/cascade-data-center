## City ArcGIS zoning record

The City zoning map identifies the site area as:

- **Zoning classification:** Heavy Industrial
- **Zone code:** **M-2**

The selected zoning feature displays **PIN 1932127004** and reports a last-edit date of **July 19, 2022**. That is not the current Simple Mining parcel number.

Dubuque County now identifies the property as **PIN 1932176027, Lot 2 Cascade Industrial Park 14th Addition**. The most supportable working interpretation is that the City zoning layer retains an older/predecessor parcel reference while the county parcel layer reflects the later replat/current parcel configuration. This is an inference from the two public records, not a City-issued legal interpretation.

For project work:

- use **Dubuque County PIN 1932176027** for current parcel identity, boundary, acreage, ownership, sale, and assessment;
- use the **City ArcGIS zoning map** for the **M-2 Heavy Industrial** district designation;
- do **not** use PIN 1932127004 as the current Simple Mining parcel identifier merely because it remains in the older zoning-layer attribute.

## Relationship to the October 1 project concept

At the October 1 community forum, Simple Mining described the site as approximately **15.2 gross acres**. The county record now independently corroborates that figure at **15.22 acres**.

Earlier March 2026 reporting described an approximately four-acre containerized mining installation. The current county acreage shows why that early physical-use figure should not be treated as the size of the property purchased: the company owns a substantially larger 15.22-acre parcel, while any particular building, equipment yard, substation, cooling system, parking, setbacks, stormwater facilities, screening, and final site plan would occupy only portions of the tract.

The October 1 building and infrastructure concept remains preliminary. The parcel ownership and acreage are now independently documented.

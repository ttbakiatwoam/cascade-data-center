ORDINANCE 4A-10

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 165.11.R-2.E “MINIMUM LOT AREA,
WIDTH” OF CHAPTER 165 “ZONING ORDINANCE” OF THE CASCADE CODE OF
ORDINANCES REDUCING THE MIMIMUM LOT AREA AND WIDTH
REQUIREMENTS FOR ZERO-LOT LINE SINGLE FAMILY ATTACHED DEWLLINGS
IN THE R-2 ZONING DISTRICT

BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of the City of Cascade, Iowa as follows:

SECTION 1: AMENDED SECTION. That Section 165.11.R-2.E “Minimum Lot
Area, Width” for zero-lot line single family attached dwellings is hereby changed from 6,000
square feet of area and 60-feet of width to 4,500 square feet of area and 45-feet of width.

E. Minimum Lot Area, Width      Minimum Yard Requirements      Maximum Height

Zero-Lot line single-family     Zero-Lot line single-family     2½ stories or
attached dwellings              attached dwellings              35-feet

Area:   4,500 sq.ft.            Front:          25-feet
Width:  45-feet                 Rear:           25-feet
Depth:  100-feet                Interior Side:  0-feet
                                Exterior Side:  8-feet
                                Corner Lot:     25-feet

SECTION 2. EFFECTIVE DATE. This ordinance shall be in effect from and after
its final passage, approval publication as required by law.

PASSED, ADOPTED, and APPROVED this 10th day of May 2010.

Greg Staner, Mayor

Attest:
Randy Lansing, City Administrator/Clerk

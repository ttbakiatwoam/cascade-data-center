**<u>ORDINANCE \#02-22</u>**

**AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE CODE OF ORDINANCES OF THE CITY OF CASCADE, IOWA, BY AMENDING  
  
CHAPTER 6-12 BUILDING PERMITS**

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ENACTED, by the City Council of the City of Cascade, Iowa, as follows:

Section I. <u>Section Modified</u>. Title VI Physical Environment, Chapter 12 Building Permits of the Code of Ordinances of the City of Cascade, Iowa, is repealed and the following adopted in lieu thereof:

##  CHAPTER 12 BUILDING PERMITS

6-12-1 Purpose

6-12-2 Structure Defined

6-12-3 Permit Required

6-12-4 Application

6-12-5 Fees

6-12-6 Plans Required

6-12-7 Location of Structure

6-12-8 Building Code Adopted

~~  
~~6-12-9 Fences

6-12-10 Curb Cuts

6-12-11 Authority of City Council

6-12-12 Permit Issued

6-12-13 Limitations on Permit

6-12-8 BUILDING CODE ADOPTED. There is hereby adopted by reference as the “Cascade Building Code” the current State of Iowa Building Code. The “Cascade Building Code” shall be controlling in the construction of buildings and other structures in all matters covered by such building code within the corporate limits of the City. Inspections shall be as set forth by resolution of the Council.

6-12-9 FENCES. No setback requirements shall be applicable to the construction of a fence.

6-12-10 CURB CUTS. No curb cut shall be constructed or permitted without first obtaining a building permit.

6-12-11 AUTHORITY OF CITY COUNCIL. The City Council shall have full authority to accept or reject any plans and specifications submitted.

6-12-12 PERMIT ISSUED. Permits shall be issued by the Zoning Administrator and/or staff delegated to and hired via a contract for services entered into with City Council approval, in duplicate, one copy for the applicant and one copy to be retained in the City records.

6-12-13 LIMITATIONS ON PERMIT.

Section II. <u>Severability Clause</u>. If any section, provision or part of the ordinance shall be adjudged invalid or unconstitutional, such adjudication shall not affect the validity of the ordinance or any section, provision or part thereof not adjudged invalid or unconstitutional.

Section III. <u>When Effective</u>. This ordinance shall be in effect from and after its final passage, approval and publication as provided by law.

PASSED, ADOPTED AND APPROVED this 22<sup>nd</sup> day of August, 2022.

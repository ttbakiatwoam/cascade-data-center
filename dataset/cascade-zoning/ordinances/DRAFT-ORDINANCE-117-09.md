DRAFT ORDINANCE 117-09

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING CHAPTER 165.11 “DISTRICT REGULATIONS FOR
M-1 & M-2” AND CHAPTER 165.41 “DEFINITIONS” OF THE CASCADE ZONING
ORDINANCE BY ALLOWING ZERO-LOT LINE BUILDINGS IN THE M-1 AND M-2
ZONING DISTRICTS AND DEFINING ZERO-LOT LINE BUILDINGS

BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of the City of Cascade, Iowa as follows:

Section 1: That the following definition of is hereby added to Article 4, Chapter 165.41
of the Zoning Ordinance of the City of Cascade:

“8. A”    Building, Zero-Lot Line: A building which physically adjoins one other
building across an abutting side lot line, with each building occupying its own parcel.

Section 2: That the following “Minimum Lot Area & Lot Width”, “Minimum Yard
Requirements”, and Maximum Height” are hereby added to Article 2, Chapter 165.11. “M-1
Light Industrial” of the Zoning Ordinance of the City of Cascade:

E. Minimum Lot Area, Width      Minimum Yard Requirements      Maximum Height
None.                           Zero-Lot line Buildings.        60-feet.
                                Front: ................. 25-feet
                                Rear: .................. 25-feet
                                Interior Side: ......... 0-feet
                                Exterior Side: ........ 20-feet
                                Corner Lot: ............ 20-feet

Section 3: That the following “Minimum Lot Area & Lot Width”, “Minimum Yard
Requirements”, and Maximum Height” are hereby added to Article 2, Chapter 165.11. “M-2
Heavy Industrial” of the Zoning Ordinance of the City of Cascade:

E. Minimum Lot Area, Width      Minimum Yard Requirements      Maximum Height
None.                           Zero-Lot line Buildings.        60-feet.
                                Front: ................. 25-feet
                                Rear: .................. 25-feet
                                Interior Side: ......... 0-feet
                                Exterior Side: ........ 20-feet
                                Corner Lot: ............ 20-feet

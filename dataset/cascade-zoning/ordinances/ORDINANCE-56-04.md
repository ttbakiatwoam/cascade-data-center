ORDINANCE 56-04

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING CHAPTER 165 “ZONING ORDINANCE” OF THE
CASCADE CODE OF ORDINANCES BY ADDING SECTION 165.11.R-1.E.2 “MINIMUM
YARD REQUIREMENTS FOR SINGLE LOTS OF RECORD WITH LESS THAN 80 FEET
OF FRONTAGE”

WHEREAS, the Cascade Zoning Board of Adjustment has entertained numerous setback
variance requests from homeowners of single family residential lots with less than 80 feet of
frontage to construct house additions and make common home improvements, and

WHEREAS, it is often very difficult for owners of residential lots with less than 80 feet
of frontage to document and prove hardship under the strict definition of hardship in the
Cascade Zoning Ordinance,

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of the City of Cascade,
Iowa as follows:

Section 1. That Section 165.11.R-1.E. of the Cascade Zoning of Ordinance is hereby
amended by added Section 165.11.R-1.E.2 “Minimum Yard Requirements for Single Lots of
Record with less than 80 Feet of Frontage” which has read as:

E.2.  The minimum yard requirements for an existing
      principal structure on a single lot of record platted
      prior to 1978 and having less than 80 feet of frontage
      shall be as follows:

      Front Yard - 12.5-feet or the average of all the other
          houses on the block, which ever is greater.
      Rear Yard  - 12.5-feet.
      Side Yard  - 4-feet.
      Corner Lot - 12.5-feet from the street side, provided it
          does not violate Section 165.12.1.

      Any building addition onto the principal structure in
      the above listed setbacks shall have a walkway passage
      or a common internal wall with the principal structure.

Section 2. This ordinance amendment shall take effect upon publication.

PASSED, ADOPTED, and APPROVED this 11th day of October 2004.

Rich Knepper, Mayor

Attest:
Patty Greenwood, City Clerk

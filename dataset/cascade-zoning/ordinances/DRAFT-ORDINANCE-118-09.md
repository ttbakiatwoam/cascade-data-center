DRAFT ORDINANCE 118-09

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING CHAPTER 165.11 “DISTRICT REGULATIONS” FOR
THE A-1 AGRICULTURAL AND CHAPTER 165.41 “DEFINITINS” OF THE
CASCADE ZONING ORDINANCE BY LIMITING THE NUMBER OF LIVESTOCK
UNITS ALLOWED IN THE A-1 ZONING DISTRICTS AND DEFINING LIVESTOCK

BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of the City of Cascade, Iowa as follows:

Section 1: That the following definition of is hereby added to Article 4, Chapter 165.41
of the Zoning Ordinance of the City of Cascade:

“28. A”    Livestock: Animals belonging to the bovine (cow), caprine (sheep/goat),
equine (horse), ovine or porcine (swine) species, or poultry.

Section 2: That the following “Permitted Principal Uses and Structures” are hereby
added to Article 2, Chapter 165.11. “A-1 Agricultural District” B.1.1 of the Zoning Ordinance of
the City of Cascade:

B. Permitted Principal Uses                      Minimum Required
   And Structures                                Off-Street Parking

1.1 Livestock rearing with the following         None.
    limitations:
    - Bovine, 2 head per acre.
    - Caprine, 4 head per acre.
    - Equine, 2 head per acre.
    - Ovine or porcine, 4 head per acre.
    - Poultry, 12 head per acre.

PASSED, ADOPTED, and APPROVED this _____ day of __________ 2009.

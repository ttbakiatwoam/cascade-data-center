# Draft email: Utility Consumption Records for the Data Center Advisory Committee

- **Date prepared:** November 3, 2026
- **Recipient:** Diana, City of Cascade
- **Subject:** Utility Consumption Records for the Data Center Advisory Committee
- **Status:** Draft for committee-related correspondence; not a record of delivery or a City response.

---

Diana,

I understand you're already working on collecting utility information for the Data Center Advisory Committee, which is great. I wanted to provide a comprehensive list of the information I believe the committee will need to properly evaluate Cascade's current infrastructure, historical utility consumption, and future development considerations.

**There's no need to provide this information to me individually.** If you're already gathering these records for the committee, including them in our committee materials or presenting them at an upcoming meeting is perfectly fine. I'm happy to review everything alongside the other members.

**1. Cascade Municipal Utilities (CMU) – Ten-Year Electrical History**

For 2016 through 2025, plus 2026 year-to-date where available:

- Total annual electricity purchased, generated, and sold, in kWh or MWh.
- Annual and monthly peak electrical demand in MW.
- Total annual wholesale electricity costs and retail revenues.
- Annual customer counts by residential, commercial, industrial, and municipal classifications.
- Annual system losses and existing electrical capacity information.
- Any historical demand studies or system capacity reports.

The federal Energy Information Administration maintains CMU electricity reporting records covering 2014 through 2024.

For reference, the EIA's 2024 report lists:

- **1,201 customers**
- **18,064 MWh** of annual electricity sales
- **$1,821,500** in annual retail electricity revenue
- **10.08 cents per kWh** average retail price

Source: [U.S. EIA, 2024 Utility Bundled Retail Sales, Table 10](https://www.eia.gov/electricity/sales_revenue_price/pdf/table_10.pdf)

Historical records: https://www.eia.gov/electricity/data/eia861/

It would be helpful to reconcile the federal reports against CMU's actual purchases, consumption, and peak demand. If the city has its original federal submissions from 2014 through 2024, those would also be useful.

**2. Maquoketa Valley Electric Cooperative (MVEC)**

For MVEC customers located within Cascade's corporate limits, covering 2021 through 2025:

- Complete customer list, including residential, commercial, industrial, and municipal accounts, where disclosure is permitted.
- Annual electrical consumption by customer.
- Total annual electricity charges by customer.
- Monthly usage and peak demand, where available.
- Applicable rate classifications.
- Aggregate electricity consumption and peak demand within city limits.

I recognize MVEC operates independently of the city. Any available records or information MVEC can provide would help establish a complete picture of electricity consumption within Cascade, rather than looking at CMU customers alone.

**3. CMU Customer Consumption**

For 2021 through 2025:

- Annual electricity consumption and billing totals by customer.
- Monthly usage and demand figures, where available.
- Existing reports identifying the 30 largest commercial and industrial electricity consumers.
- Customer classifications and applicable rates.

Where individual customer information is confidential, anonymized or aggregated consumption data would still be helpful.

**4. Municipal Water Consumption**

For 2021 through 2025:

- The 30 largest commercial and industrial water consumers.
- Annual gallons consumed and annual billing totals.
- Monthly consumption, where available.
- Total annual citywide water production and consumption.
- Annual water system capacity and peak-demand information, where available.

Again, anonymized data is acceptable wherever customer confidentiality applies.

**5. City-Owned Facilities and Infrastructure**

I'd also like the committee to have five years of electricity and water usage, including associated annual costs, for all city-owned and operated infrastructure.

Specifically:

- Cascade Municipal Swimming Pool, including water used for filling and operation, electrical consumption, and annual utility costs.
- City Hall and municipal offices.
- Public library and community facilities.
- Police, fire, and emergency services facilities where the city pays utilities.
- Public works buildings, garages, and maintenance facilities.
- Municipal wells, pumping stations, water treatment facilities, and water towers.
- Wastewater treatment facilities and sewage lift stations.
- Streetlights, traffic controls, and other public electrical infrastructure.
- Parks, recreation facilities, and other municipal properties.
- Any additional city-owned accounts or facilities not listed above.

It would be helpful to identify individual facilities and their consumption rather than having everything combined into one municipal total. This should include facilities receiving electricity from MVEC as well as CMU.

**6. Committee Records and Presentation**

Existing spreadsheets, reports, billing summaries, or utility exports should be sufficient. There's no need to create entirely new reports if the information is already available.

I also recognize that Iowa law limits the disclosure of certain utility customer information. Any legally available aggregate or anonymized data should serve the committee's purposes.

I don't expect you to duplicate work you're already doing. My intention is simply to make sure we have a comprehensive set of information available to the entire committee.

I believe these records are necessary for us to understand Cascade's actual utility demand, infrastructure capacity, and operating costs before making recommendations that could affect future industrial development.

Having this information presented to the entire committee will also help ensure we're all working from the same verified figures rather than differing assumptions.

Thank you for everything you're doing to help move this process forward.

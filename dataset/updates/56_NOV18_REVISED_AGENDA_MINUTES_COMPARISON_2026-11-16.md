# November 16, 2026 — Three versions of the November 18 agenda and November 11 draft minutes

**Independent primary-source comparison.** Three 107-page scanned packets were provided by a participant. The initial 10:54 a.m. version was already reviewed; two later packets were received with this follow-up. The creation times below are **embedded PDF metadata**, not verified City website publication or delivery times. The first three pages were compared visually; the reproduced research and appendices are **not certified identical line-by-line** across revisions.

## The finding

**The agenda was corrected twice, but the November 11 minutes were not substantively corrected.** The revised agenda now describes the four November 11 definitions as **"Approved"** while simultaneously calling for **"Reapprove"** on November 18. It gives weight to the participant's prior account that the committee took affirmative action November 11, but it does **not** authenticate the vote totals or individual votes. The attached November 11 draft minutes still say the four definitions **will be voted on again** and do not document their November 11 motions/tallies.

**Interpretation:** Changing **"Discussed" to "Approved"** acknowledges the existence of approvals in the agenda wording. The operative verb **"Reapprove"** still leaves the purpose and procedural effect of a second vote unspecified. Removal of **Setbacks** narrows the listed subjects; it does not, on this document alone, establish why the item was removed or exclude all related discussion under "Others."

## November 11 minutes — page 2 (both revisions still deficient)

The visually reviewed text still says that:

- The committee **"reviewed definitions for updating the M-2 zoning district"** including the four names, then reproduces their wording;
- **"Due to technical issues, the recording of the meeting stopped at 8:00pm. Anything that happened after that was not recorded. At the November 18th meeting, the above 4 definitions will be again voted on by the advisory committee."**
- The minutes nevertheless record an **adjournment motion at 9:30 p.m.** by Saunders, seconded by Weber, **all ayes**.

**No separate November 11 motion, vote result or member-specific roll-call information is recorded for the four definition approvals** in the page 2 draft. This is a failure of documentary completeness in the supplied *draft minutes*; it does not independently establish who caused it or that the City approved those minutes. The agenda now recognizes prior approvals, but reapproval on November 18 would not by itself reconstruct the precise November 11 voting record.

## The four definitions — page 3

The four bodies and names appear unchanged across the initial and two revised packets:

- Data Center
- Data Center Business
- Sustainable Design
- Hyperscale Data Center: a Data Center meeting **both** (1) a facility **100,000 square feet or larger**, and (2) **electrical demand exceeding 60 megawatts**.

The participant's original account reports contested vote tallies for Data Center and Data Center Business as **uncertain**, Sustainable Design as **unanimous**, and Hyperscale as **5–3**. These figures are **not verified by the revised City's draft minutes**.

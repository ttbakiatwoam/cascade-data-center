# Canonical November 11, 2026 committee-approved definitions — Cascade zoning working drafts

**Source status:** Verbatim substance as corrected and reported by a November 11 committee participant. Four definitions were approved for the advisory committee's draft; **this is not independently certified against the City's signed minutes**, and **no City Council ordinance was enacted** by these votes. Treat the approved wording as the single editing source for all **current** ordinance proposals. Historical drafts are archived unaltered.

## Approved operative definition wording

**Data Center:** A structure or building rehabilitated or constructed to house a group of networked server computers in one physical location in order to centralize the storage, management, and dissemination of data and information pertaining to a particular business, taxonomy, or body of knowledge. A data center business’s facility typically includes the mechanical and electrical systems, redundant or backup power supplies, redundant data communications connections, environmental controls, and fire suppression systems.

**Data Center Business:** An entity whose business among other businesses, is to operate or lease a data center in the city of cascade.

**Sustainable Design:** Construction design that reduces negative environmental effects and improves occupant health and comfort through resource conservation, waste minimization, and healthier environments.

**Hyperscale Data Center:** A Data Center meeting both of the following conditions:

1. **100,000 square feet or larger.**
2. **Electrical demand exceeding 60 megawatts.**

**Hyperscale approval:** **5–3** (participant reported). The square-footage **AND** the electrical-demand test are both required. Exactly 100,000 square feet meets the first threshold; exactly 60 MW does **not** exceed the second. The user clarified that **“annually” was not in the approved wording**, and the term was **“Hyperscale Data Center,”** not “Hyper-Data Center.” The connector “A Data Center meeting both of the following conditions” is explanatory ordinance drafting that faithfully represents the two approved tests; exact punctuation and opening phrase remain subject to the official minutes.

**Other votes:** Data Center and Data Center Business both passed; exact vote counts remain **5–3 or 6–2 (unconfirmed)**. Sustainable Design passed **unanimously**; do not assume 8–0 without confirming all members voting. These are committee recommendations for a draft, **not binding City zoning**.

## Existing state law used only as source and legal comparator

- [Iowa Code §423.3(95)(f)(1)](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/2026/423.3.pdf): state **sales-tax** “data center” comparator. The committee's text is not an exact copy and does not import sales-tax eligibility into zoning.
- [Iowa Code §423.3(95)(f)(2)](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/2026/423.3.pdf): “data center business” sales-tax comparator. The committee wording adds the local reference “in the city of cascade.”
- [Iowa Code §103A.3(25)](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/2026/103A.3.pdf): state building-code sustainable-design terminology. The committee wording is a paraphrase, not verbatim statutory language.
- **Hyperscale Data Center:** proposed **local** classification from the committee vote; not an existing Iowa Code definition.
- **City of Cascade §165.41:** already defines Building/Structure, Lot, Dwelling Unit and Accessory Use; leave these in force. Iowa Code §103A.3(5) defines Construction for the state building-code program. Do not add unnecessary, duplicate zoning definitions.

## Revision discipline for every CURRENT ordinance

1. Repeat the four definition bodies **without changing their voted thresholds, “AND,” or approved words**. Remove parenthetical vote tallies and “committee-approved” from enactable term headwords; keep this information in noncodified notes.
2. References in proposed §165.11(B)(19) and §165.12(17) must use **Data Center** and **Hyperscale Data Center** as defined in proposed §165.41. Other project-only industry terms must not replace them.
3. **Do not** insert a calendar-year period, “annually,” gross-floor-area measurement, alternative “OR” trigger, extra micro-data-center category, or a special exception that permits Hyperscale Data Centers unless the committee separately votes on the needed changes.
4. A committee vote approving the **definition** of Hyperscale Data Center does **not** constitute approval of the separately proposed citywide **prohibition**. Both must be distinguished in the redline and sources.
5. The 86-term research glossary and old PDFs are reference archives, not alternative current municipal definitions.

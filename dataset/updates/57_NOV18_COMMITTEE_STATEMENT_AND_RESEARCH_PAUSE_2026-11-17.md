## Statement as prepared

Deanna,

Please read the following statement in its entirety at the November 18, 2026, Data Center Advisory Committee meeting and include it in the official meeting record.

**Statement to the Data Center Advisory Committee**

Effective immediately, I will no longer be attending the regular meetings of the Data Center Advisory Committee.

It has become increasingly apparent that the decisions before us need to be made by the elected leadership of the City of Cascade. The committee has been provided sufficient information to fulfill its advisory role, and the responsibility for determining the City's direction ultimately rests with the City Council.

Over the past several months, I have invested considerable personal time researching Iowa law, reviewing ordinances adopted or proposed by municipalities throughout Iowa and the region, evaluating technical and environmental considerations, and preparing multiple comprehensive drafts of an M-2 zoning ordinance. I have provided extensive supporting documentation, legal references, comparative research, and proposed language intended to establish a fair, reasonable, and enforceable framework for regulating data centers.

The committee now has more than sufficient information to make an informed recommendation to the City Council. Additional discussion without meaningful progress does not justify the continued commitment of personal time, particularly time that would otherwise be spent with my family.

**To be absolutely clear, I am not resigning from the Data Center Advisory Committee.** I remain a member and am willing to review and cast my vote on a proposed ordinance once the committee has completed its review of the information and drafts already provided. However, I will no longer participate in regular meetings that do not advance that objective.

The City of Cascade has an obligation to its residents, businesses, and prospective developers to establish clear and legally defensible standards. I encourage the committee to utilize the substantial work already completed and, **without unnecessary debate and delays**, finalize its recommendations and present an ordinance to the City Council for consideration.

I have fulfilled my responsibility to this committee by providing extensive research, proposed solutions, and a willingness to work toward a reasonable outcome. At this point, my continued attendance offers no additional benefit to the work already accomplished, the City's leadership, or my family.

I remain committed to seeing a responsible ordinance adopted and will participate when the committee is prepared to take substantive action on a final proposal.

Respectfully,

Andrew Saunders  
Member, Data Center Advisory Committee  
Cascade, Iowa

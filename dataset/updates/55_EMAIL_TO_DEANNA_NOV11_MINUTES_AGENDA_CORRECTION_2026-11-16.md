# November 16, 2026 — Draft email to Deanna: November 11 votes and November 18 agenda

**Status:** Draft prepared at the author's direction. **Not sent**, receipt not verified. Intended recipient: Deanna, City of Cascade staff. This is author correspondence, not a certified City record.

**Subject:** Correction Needed: November 11 Committee Votes and November 18 Agenda

Deanna,

I reviewed the November 18 Data Center Advisory Committee agenda and packet, including the draft minutes for November 11. I am concerned that the minutes and agenda misrepresent what occurred at our November 11 meeting.

The draft minutes state that because the recording stopped at approximately 8:00 p.m., the committee will vote again on the four definitions at the November 18 meeting. Agenda item 6 then calls for approval of the four definitions discussed on November 11. **Those definitions were not merely discussed. The committee voted on and approved them for inclusion in its working ordinance draft.**

To be clear, my recollection of the November 11 actions is:

- **Data Center:** approved for the committee's working draft. The exact split vote needs to be reconciled with members' notes.
- **Data Center Business:** approved for the working draft. The exact split vote needs to be reconciled with members' notes.
- **Sustainable Design:** approved unanimously.
- **Hyperscale Data Center:** approved **5–3**, with **both** criteria required: a facility of **100,000 square feet or larger AND electrical demand exceeding 60 megawatts**. The word "annually" was not voted into this definition.

The five recurring affirmative voters on the contested motions were Mike Delaney, Mark Weber, Andrew Saunders, Gayle Walter and Doug Frey. Michael Rogers and Dave Mehrl voted yes on some motions, while Rob McNally consistently voted no on contested motions. This is my general recollection of the pattern, **not a substitute for a verified member-by-member tally for each separate motion**. The other proposed definitions were largely skipped because of existing Iowa or Cascade definitions; they should not be recorded as having been defeated by votes.

A recording failure does not make motions taken in an open meeting disappear. **Iowa Code §21.3(2) requires the governmental body to keep minutes reflecting its actions and sufficient information to show each member's vote.** The law places that obligation on the committee as a body, regardless of who is assigned to prepare the minutes. If the chairman did not take adequate notes or did not ensure that the votes he presided over were documented, **that is a failure to carry out his recordkeeping responsibilities, not a failure of the committee to vote**. It would be inappropriate to treat an administrative or recordkeeping failure as though the votes never occurred.

There is also an inconsistency in the draft minutes: although they say the recording stopped at 8:00 p.m., they nevertheless document the 9:30 p.m. adjournment motion and unanimous vote. If an action after the recording cutoff can be preserved in the minutes, I would like an explanation of why the four intervening definition votes cannot be reconstructed from the chair's, staff's and members' contemporaneous notes and recollections.

Please correct the draft November 11 minutes **before the committee is asked to approve them under agenda item 5**. Please also clarify or correct item 6 so it reflects the fact that these were **previously approved committee-draft definitions**, rather than implying no vote occurred. If a new vote is being proposed as a separate reconsideration or replacement action, that should be stated explicitly, with its purpose and procedural basis, rather than quietly substituting it for the November 11 record.

Finally, agenda item 7 categorizes emissions, operational noise, environmental issues and setbacks as definitions. Those are also substantive operating and land-use standards. Please clarify whether the committee is reviewing proposed definitions, proposing enforceable M-2 performance standards, or both, and identify any intended action accordingly.

Please circulate the corrected minutes and agenda clarification to the committee before the November 18 meeting, and preserve this correspondence with the relevant committee materials. I am available to compare my recollection with other members' notes to ensure that the actual votes are recorded accurately.

Thank you,

Andrew Saunders

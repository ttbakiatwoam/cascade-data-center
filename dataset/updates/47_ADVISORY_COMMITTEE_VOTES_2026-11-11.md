# November 11, 2026 — reported definition votes and next meeting

**Status:** Participant-reported meeting outcome, recorded November 11, 2026. Exact counts and official-minute approval **not independently verified**. This is not a legislative adoption of the M-2 ordinance.

**Data center** — approved for inclusion in the draft; tally **5–3 or 6–2, uncertain**:

> “Data center” means a structure or building rehabilitated or constructed to house a group of networked server computers in one physical location in order to centralize the storage, management, and dissemination of data and information pertaining to a particular business, taxonomy, or body of knowledge. A data center business’s facility typically includes the mechanical and electrical systems, redundant or backup power supplies, redundant data communications connections, environmental controls, and fire suppression systems.

State source comparator: [Iowa Code §423.3(95)(f)(1)](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/2026/423.3.pdf) (sales/use tax definition, with material textual differences; not itself municipal zoning law).

**Data center business** — approved for inclusion in the draft; tally **5–3 or 6–2, uncertain**:

> “Data center business” means an entity whose business among other businesses, is to operate or lease a data center in the city of cascade.

State source comparator: [Iowa Code §423.3(95)(f)(2)](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/2026/423.3.pdf) (tax-program definition; local text adds “in the city of cascade”).

**Sustainable design** — **unanimously** approved; the numeric tally was not independently established:

> “Sustainable design” Construction design that reduces negative environmental effects and improves occupant health and comfort through resource conservation, waste minimization, and healthier environments.

State source comparator: [Iowa Code §103A.3(25)](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/2026/103A.3.pdf). The local wording is a *paraphrase*, **not** a verbatim copy of statutory text. Related statute: [§103A.8B](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/2026/103A.8B.pdf). A definition does not itself impose a performance duty.

**Hyperscale Data Center** — **approved for inclusion in the draft, participant-reported vote 5–3**:

The two **approved thresholds**, as clarified by the committee participant, were:

- **100,000 square feet or larger**
- **Electrical demand exceeding 60 megawatts**

Both conditions apply to a Data Center; **100,000 square feet exactly meets** the size threshold, but **60 MW exactly does not exceed** the electrical-demand threshold. This proposed local classification does not come from Iowa Code §423.3. The participant's correction supersedes prior project transcriptions that said “more than 100,000 square feet” or included **“annually.”** The term voted on was **Hyperscale Data Center**, not “Hyper-Data Center.” Official minutes and final precise ordinance punctuation remain pending verification.

**Future measurement proposal, not approved November 11:** The term “electrical demand” needs an objective determination method. A new requirement to evaluate it **by calendar year (January 1–December 31)** is a substantive clarification and **would require a separate committee vote**. The 5–3 approval did **not** establish a calendar-year measurement period. MW is a measure of power, not MWh consumption.

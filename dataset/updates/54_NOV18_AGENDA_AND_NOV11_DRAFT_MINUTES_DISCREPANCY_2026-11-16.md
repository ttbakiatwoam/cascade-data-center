## Legal framework

- [2026 Iowa Code §21.3(2)](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/2026/21.3.pdf) requires **each governmental body** to keep minutes showing time, place, attendees, actions, the result of each vote and information sufficient to indicate each member's vote.
- [Iowa Public Information Board 20AO:0006](https://ipib.iowa.gov/notes-open-meeting) expressly states the legal duty is on the governmental body, not exclusively the staff member delegated to write minutes.
- [Iowa Public Information Board 24AO:0001](https://ipib.iowa.gov/24ao0001-chapter-21-requirements-work-sessions) explains that open sessions generally are **not required to be audio recorded**.
- Lack of a recording **does not void or prove the absence of a motion**. Conversely, these participant accounts are not certified minutes. Reconstruct with corroboration from members, chair, staff, documentary evidence or additional recordings. Treat later reconsideration as a separately identified action if proposed.
- Do not assert that the chair alone bears the statute's duty without evidence of the chair's delegated responsibilities. If tasked with vote recordkeeping and failing, that would be an accountability issue, not proof the committee did not act.

## What this means for the working M-2 proposal

- The committee recorded a focus on **M-2 permitted-use rules and definitions**. The October 28 record contains **no ordinance adoption vote** and no recommendation approving or denying Simple Mining or another specific project.
- The present [November 3 proposed M-2 performance standards](../../proposals/DRAFT-ORDINANCE-M2-DATA-CENTER-PERFORMANCE-STANDARDS-2026-11-03.md) remain **independent draft recommendations**, not committee-adopted language or enacted ordinance.
- The [November 3 definitions comparison](../policy/38_IOWA_STATE_AND_M2_DEFINITIONS_2026-11-03.md) provides candidate definitions and cites for member review; it should not be presented as the committee's agreed definitions.
- Questions for the City Attorney and unresolved record issues should be identified precisely, not inferred from the short minutes.

> **STATUS: DRAFT / WORKING DOCUMENT — NOT ADOPTED LAW.**
>
> Jackson County, Iowa labels the documents on its zoning-ordinance page **“HDCF Working Drafts - For Discussion Purposes.”** As of October 6, 2026, the newest version listed by the County is dated **August 17, 2026**.

> This document should be cited only as a **Jackson County working draft** and not as an enacted ordinance, final recommendation, or statement of current law.

## Citation rule for this repository

When comparing Jackson County with Cascade or other Iowa jurisdictions:

1. label Jackson County language as **draft** unless a later official record establishes adoption;
2. identify the **draft date/version** when quoting a provision;
3. do not assume language in the June 15 mirror remains unchanged in the August 17 draft; and
4. check the official Jackson County zoning page for a newer version before relying on a specific requirement.

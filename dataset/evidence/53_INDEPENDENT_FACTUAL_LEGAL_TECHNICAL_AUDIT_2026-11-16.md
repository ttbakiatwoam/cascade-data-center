## Corrections to the audit's own analytical assumptions

1. **No automatic NOx Title V conclusion from 132 short tons of NMHC+NOx.** The calculation in the PDF used an aggregate engine certification limit for NMHC **plus** NOx, but the federal major-source threshold requires proper species-based accounting under the specific program. The combined value is a screening concern, not a legal NOx PTE determination. See [Iowa 567 IAC 22.1](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/iac/chapter/567.22.pdf), [40 CFR 60 Subpart IIII](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/part-60/subpart-IIII) and [EPA stationary engines](https://www.epa.gov/stationary-engines).
2. **The 500-hour Iowa default cannot automatically be assigned to a brand-new fleet.** Iowa's PTE rule makes that scenario conditional upon historical operation under 500 hours for five years or an enforceable limit; other rules and DNR determinations apply. It is **not** permission to run an emergency engine for 500 hours. See [567 IAC 22.1](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/iac/chapter/567.22.pdf) and [40 CFR 60.4211(f)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.4211).
3. **Fuel sulfur is engine-category-specific.** [40 CFR 60.4207(b)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.4207) refers <30 L/cylinder diesel engines to [40 CFR 1090.305](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1090.305) (generally 15 ppm sulfur by weight); §60.4207(d) specifies 1,000 ppm maximum per gallon for engines >=30 L/cylinder, subject to any stricter controlling requirement. Do **not** state that 15 ppm is the only ppm limit in the federal stationary-diesel regime.
4. **Local hypotheticals are not applicant facts.** Preliminary ~40 MW and ~15-engine descriptions do not establish final nameplate horsepower, displacement, installation date, emergency classification, state permit status, or emissions.
5. **Hyperscale scope is a policy question, not license to amend a voted definition.** A facility that does not meet **both** committee-reported criteria remains outside that proposed class. The Council has not enacted a hyperscale ban and the committee did not vote to approve the independently proposed ban.

## Actionable unverified items

- **Developer / DNR:** engine list with unit count, rated mechanical horsepower and electric MW, engine model year/serial, emissions certificate and control technology, displacement (L/cylinder), diesel specification, heat input, testing/dispatch plan, potential to emit by pollutant, construction and operating permits or exemption findings, monitoring and simultaneous operation scenarios.
- **Environment:** fuel/oil storage totals and conditional federal SPCC analysis (40 CFR Part 112); stormwater disturbance and Iowa permit coverage; applicable hazardous chemical/Tier II inventory and thresholds under 40 CFR Part 370; fire department equipment access and emergency-response studies; BESS technology and adopted local codes; refrigerants and cooling-water plans. These are conditional screening questions, **not assertions that every law applies**.
- **Local governance:** City-approved vote minutes; current consolidated ordinance and lawful administrative responsibilities; franchise ordinance and agreement; actual utility tariff and site boundary; executed project agreement/acoustic terms.
- **Legal research:** docket-by-docket verification of litigation summaries; current enactment status for Central City and other comparator ordinances; municipal attorney review of the zoning restrictions.

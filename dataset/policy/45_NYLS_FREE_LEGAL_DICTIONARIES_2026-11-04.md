# Free legal dictionaries selected for Cascade M-2 research

**Reviewed November 4, 2026.** This is a source-provenance record accompanying the [86-term state and M-2 definitions comparison](38_IOWA_STATE_AND_M2_DEFINITIONS_2026-11-03.md). It is research commentary, **not a substitution for Iowa statutes, administrative rules, enacted zoning code, or legal counsel**.

## Starting reference: New York Law School

[New York Law School Mendik Library, *Free & Low Cost Legal Research: Free Online Legal Dictionaries & Glossaries*](https://libguides.nyls.edu/lowcostlegalresearch/freedict) provides a curated list of no-subscription sources. The page, updated August 24, 2026, explains why legal senses can differ from general dictionary senses and cautions that online dictionaries may become outdated. It lists **Wex**, **FindLaw Legal Dictionary**, **Law.com**, **Farlex/TheFreeDictionary**, **Justia**, **Merriam-Webster's Law Dictionary**, and **Nolo**, among other resources. Only sources for which usable entries could be reviewed are included as active legal-dictionary table columns.

## Quality rules

1. Cite primary **Iowa Code** and **Iowa Administrative Code** definitions first; supplemental dictionaries are nonbinding.
2. Label a cell **direct**, **verified subentry**, or **related article**. Never assert a technical multiword term is a dictionary headword on the strength of a generic article.
3. A dash means **not verified in this review**, not proof that the word is absent from the dictionary.
4. Use **original editorial paraphrases**, not verbatim copying of a dictionary's copyrighted text.
5. Maintain Chicago-style numbered bibliography notes for the official Iowa law and the named dictionary publishers.
6. Recheck URLs and revision dates before citing a dictionary in formal legislative findings or litigation.

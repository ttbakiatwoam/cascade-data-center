# Cascade M-2 zoning: original scan versus proposed November 3 amendment

**Draft comparison | November 3, 2026 | Independent research, not City law.**

**Answer:** The proposed M-2 amendment is based on the City's archived §165.11 text, **but the archive is not a certified up-to-date consolidation**. The original scanned M-2 pages are PDF **45–49** (printed Code pages **34–38**). The October 2022 fitness-use amendment and September 2024 fencing amendment also affect how the district currently operates. Before this review the proposed G subsection accidentally began at **G(1)** even though existing G(1) already refers to supplementary district regulations. That conflict has been corrected: **existing G(1) is preserved, and 18 new performance provisions are proposed as G(2)–G(19)**.[^1][^2][^3][^7]

## Markup key and limits

| Marking | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **RETAIN — no amendment** | Text in the original scan that the new proposal does not repeal or substitute. |
| **2022 / 2024 enacted amendment** | Later municipal ordinance text verified in the source collection, but not printed within the original M-2 scan. |
| **ADD — proposed text** | New ordinance language requiring introduction, City process, approval, and publication before it could become enforceable. |
| **VERIFY — legal status** | A scanned *draft*, apparent amendment, or codification detail not established as enacted by the accessible records. |

The highlighted reproduction later in the comparison PDF marks **insertion points and affected subsections**. It **does not falsely depict the original City ordinance as containing the new language**. The PDF also includes the original scan pages **unaltered**, followed by the full November 3 amended proposal.

## Material questions for the City Attorney and City Clerk

1. **Confirm the complete enacted amendment record.** The original scanned M-2 text and the separately preserved 2022 and 2024 ordinances are supported. Other enacted amendments since the original code may affect enumeration, yards, and land use. This comparison **must not be presented as a certified codification of current M-2 law**.
2. **Confirm the 2009 zero-lot-line proposal's status.** Its copy is labeled *draft* and unsigned. A later enacted counterpart might modify side-yard requirements.
3. **Preserve G(1).** Insert the new performance requirements only as G(2)–G(19) (or the next confirmed available sequence), avoiding accidental repeal or numbering collisions.
4. **Separate E setbacks from proposed G(8).** General M-2 dimensions remain. Proposed 100-/200-ft requirements are conditioned on direct adjacency to a residential zoning district; a distant dwelling with an intervening parcel does not itself satisfy that adjacency trigger.
5. **Review special-exception authority.** A new D(8) approval requirement must be consistent with existing §165.34(2), Iowa Code §414.12, nonconforming-use protections and the City's public notice process.
6. **Apply screening law correctly.** Screening or enclosure must account for the adopted M-2 fence-height and maintenance restrictions in §165.12(12).
7. **Do not treat proposed thresholds as state limits.** The 5 MW/25,000 gallon/2 MW/5 MWh triggers, dBA/dBC noise numbers, equipment setbacks and financial security requirements are proposed local policy choices subject to counsel review and an evidentiary record.[^5][^6]

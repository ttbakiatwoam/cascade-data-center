## Definition design options relevant to the next Cascade committee discussion

1. **Function:** A facility or campus principally devoted to storing, processing or transmitting electronic data, together with associated infrastructure.
2. **Boundary:** Identify accessory on-site IT rooms, small edge equipment, and independently regulated telecom uses; distinguish exclusion from a blanket exemption for industrial impacts.
3. **Scope:** Include colocation, owner-operated cloud and other computing models without making workload/customer names the trigger.
4. **Threshold:** If a quantitative zoning tier is used, define whether it measures requested utility service, maximum coincident demand, IT load, net/server floor area, water draw, or another verifiable metric. Specify aggregation and subsequent expansion review.
5. **Uniformity:** Keep comparable M-2 performance requirements tied to measurable effects and consistent district/use classes under §414.2. Compare with the proposal's existing "Major Industrial Facility" concept, rather than adding a new data-center-only exemption.

These are **review options only**. Cascade's [November 3 draft](../../proposals/DRAFT-ORDINANCE-M2-DATA-CENTER-PERFORMANCE-STANDARDS-2026-11-03.md) already separates the statewide tax definition from local zoning, and includes Major Industrial Facility impact thresholds. The [companion definitions draft](../../proposals/DRAFT-ORDINANCE-CH165-DEFINITIONS-2026-11-03.md) remains unchanged pending a substantive drafting decision.

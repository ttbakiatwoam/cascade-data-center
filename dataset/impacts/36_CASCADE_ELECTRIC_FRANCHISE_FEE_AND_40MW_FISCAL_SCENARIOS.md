# 36 — Cascade MVEC Electric Franchise Fee, 40 MW Fiscal Scenarios, and Jobs Tax Modeling

> Reviewed October 22, 2026. **Project-generated research**, not adopted law, an actual MVEC project tariff, a City-approved franchise, a certified income-tax estimate, or a firm Simple Mining hiring commitment. Full public readable companion: [website analysis](../../docs/franchise-fee-and-jobs.md).

## Key issue and finding

Iowa Code 2026 §364.2(4) generally permits a nonexclusive city electricity franchise and a fee up to **5%** of the outside franchisee's gross revenues from *sales within city limits*. A 2024 MVEC published Rate Schedule 7 benchmark applied at continuous 40 MW models annual billed retail revenue of **$20,641,658.40 before power-cost adjustments**. Corresponding **1–5%** franchise amounts are **$206,416.58; $412,833.17; $619,249.75; $825,666.34; $1,032,082.92**.

**Unresolved prerequisites:** The site must be *inside corporate limits*; MVEC must actually make *retail* power sales to the customer; an in-force franchise and fee must be validly adopted; customer tariff and invoicing entity must be known; city-operated electric utility PILOT comparison must be addressed. None is established solely by M-2 zoning.

The City of Cascade owns its municipal utility. A city-owned utility does not owe itself franchise fees. Municipal-utility PILOT instead depends on **surplus** transfers under Iowa Code §384.89, with applicable bond/reserve restrictions. Iowa League of Cities handbook and clerk manual advise attention to parity where a city owns a utility and a cooperative also sells power inside it.

## MVEC benchmark formula, reproducible

**Tariff:** [MVEC, Master Electric Tariff, Rate Schedule 7, sheets 17-13 and 17-14, issued June 21, 2024, effective August 1, 2024](https://mvec.coop/sites/default/files/MVEC%20Master%20Tariff%20-%20June%202024.pdf). Transmission-level *Special Time of Use Large Power Service* requires special signed board-approved agreement, and qualification under CIPCO's rate schedule. No evidence it will be Simple Mining's 2026 tariff.

- Input maximum/assumed constant energy demand **40 MW = 40,000 kW**.
- Operating hours **365 × 24 = 8,760 hours/year**.
- 100% load factor energy **40,000 × 8,760 = 350,400,000 kWh**.
- Tariff energy component **38.521 mills/kWh = $0.038521/kWh**.
- Annual energy charge **350,400,000 × $0.038521 = $13,497,758.40**.
- Seasonal demand **40,000 kW × $6.54 × 12 = $3,139,200.00**.
- Monthly coincident demand **40,000 kW × $8.34 × 12 = $4,003,200.00**.
- Basic service **$125 × 12 = $1,500.00**.
- **Total = $20,641,658.40/year = ~$0.058909/kWh effective**, excluding PCA No. 4, power-factor, metering, tax, negotiated rate and other true-ups.

**Fee equation**: gross retail sales base × franchise percentage. This is not a power-consumption tax; City receipts will depend on what's included in the ordinance's gross receipt base.

If coincident demand falls, annual demand charges and resulting fee will usually also fall; this table deliberately does not predict that effect. A $0.01/kWh PCA adjustment at full annual use increases base revenue by **$3,504,000**, and city receipts by **$35,040 at 1%**, or **$175,200 at 5%**, **if** included in franchise gross receipt base.

**Municipal rate comparator only**: Cascade publishes **$0.06798/kWh + $5.59/kW monthly demand + $31/month meter** at [its city electric page](https://www.cityofcascade.org/vnews/display.v/SEC/Municipal%20Utilities%7CElectric). Naively applying that rate to the same 40MW 100%-load profile gives **$26,503,764/year**, but it is neither MVEC's price nor demonstrated municipal supply capability. It cannot be used to compute an MVEC franchise fee or an automatic municipal PILOT.

## LOST, tax-exempt data center electricity, and taxable property

Under §423B.5 franchise-taxed electricity does not also incur LOST. Under §423.3(95) a **qualifying data center may be exempt from state sales/use tax on electricity** (15-year post-2025 facility rule for municipalities under 30k, subject to all requirements). As LOST is tied to state taxability, fully exempt electricity normally has **no LOST from the start**; therefore comparing a *new* 3% fee against "existing 1% LOST on all data center electricity" would exaggerate foregone receipts. Simple Mining has **represented** intent to seek state data-center exemptions and ~$400M investment, neither exemption qualification nor taxable sales have been independently validated. A gross-receipts franchise fee is conceptually distinct from electricity state sales/use tax; confirm with legal counsel.

Property taxation/assessment is completely separate: $400M capital cost does not prove taxable assessed value, levy distribution, or incentive effects.

## Income taxes: requested 40 employees × $80,000

**Requested modeling assumption**, NOT a company commitment. October 1 forum record states **20–25 permanent jobs** projected at approximately **$75,000–$80,000 average wage** ([existing jobs note](12_JOBS_TAXES_AND_COMMUNITY_BENEFITS.md)). The hypothetical 40-worker gross payroll is **$3,200,000/year**.

For 2026 assume **each worker is single**, works in Iowa, earns $80,000 in wages only, no credits, itemized deductions, retirement contribution or extra incomes, and takes the federal **$16,100** single standard deduction ([IRS Rev. Proc. in 2025-45 IRB](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2025-45_IRB)). Federal and Iowa starting taxable income is **$63,900 each**; Iowa uses federal taxable income subject to modifications ([IDR conformity instructions](https://revenue.iowa.gov/taxes/tax-guidance/individual-income-tax/1040-expanded-instructions/conformity-irc)).

- Federal 2026 single brackets: 10% × first $12,400 = **$1,240**; 12% × next $38,000 = **$4,560**; 22% × remaining $13,500 = **$2,970**. Federal tax **$8,770/person**, **$350,800/40**.
- Iowa flat 2026 rate **3.8%** ([official IDR announcement](https://revenue.iowa.gov/press-release/2025-10-21/idr-announces-2026-individual-income-tax-and-interest-rates)): $63,900 × 0.038 = **$2,428.20/person** and **$97,128/40** absent modifications/credits.
- **Total modeled income tax = $447,928 (federal + state), $0 of it paid directly to City of Cascade**. The $121,600 shorthand (3.8% × $3.2M gross wages) overstates Iowa taxable-income-based liability.
- Social Security and Medicare employee/employer payroll taxes are separate from individual income taxes and are not City income-tax revenue. Property-tax and sales-tax effects from workers depend on who resides/spends in the city, and have not been assigned numbers.

## Public forum

Simple Mining held the public community forum concerning the proposed project in the Cascade Industrial Park on **October 1, 2026**, after accepting advance questions through **September 25, 2026**.

The raw forum transcript preserved in this repository is a WebVTT file created by **YouTube using its automated closed-captioning system**. It has not been human-verified and should not be treated as a certified verbatim transcript. The evidence-labeled forum summary is maintained in [`28_SIMPLE_MINING_COMMUNITY_FORUM_2026-10-01.md`](28_SIMPLE_MINING_COMMUNITY_FORUM_2026-10-01.md).

The public Google Form asks for:

- first and last name;
- street address / city / state / ZIP;
- a short question topic;
- the question itself.

The public form permits questions to be submitted in advance so the company can review and address them during the forum.

## Purpose of the question bank

The question bank was designed to replace generalized arguments about “data centers” with questions about the **specific Cascade proposal**.

A useful answer should, where possible, provide a number, engineering description, document, contractual commitment, responsible entity, or measurable operating limit rather than a general assurance.

## Principal information sought

### Project identity and scope

Questions seek the actual facility type, legal owner/operator, initial square footage, full buildout, phases, timeline, and future workload flexibility.

### Electricity

Questions seek initial and maximum MW demand, utility/substation/transmission needs, infrastructure-cost responsibility, ratepayer protection, and demand-response/curtailment plans.

### Cooling and water

Questions seek the exact internal cooling and external heat-rejection design, average/peak/annual water use, source water, potable-water use, wastewater/blowdown, treatment chemicals, and drought response.

### Noise

Questions seek modeled dBA at the property line and nearby residences, low-frequency/tonal analysis, major noise sources, independent studies, enforceable limits, post-construction verification, and mitigation if limits are exceeded.

### Backup generation and environment

Questions seek generator count/type/fuel/testing, emissions, stormwater/drainage effects, waste streams, electronic-waste handling, heat rejection, and willingness to fund independent environmental review.

### Jobs and local workforce

Questions distinguish temporary construction jobs from permanent full-time employment and ask for job types, pay ranges, local recruitment, and training partnerships.

### Taxes and public incentives

Questions seek taxable value, annual property-tax estimates, abatements/TIF/grants/infrastructure assistance, and clawbacks if negotiated commitments are not met.

### Infrastructure and emergency response

Questions seek road/utility impacts, truck traffic, fire suppression, batteries/UPS, fuel and hazardous materials, responder training/equipment, and responsibility for project-created public costs.

### Zoning, expansion, and enforceability

Questions seek which promises the applicant will accept as enforceable conditions, what happens if the computing workload materially changes, and whether major physical-impact changes trigger renewed City review.

### Transparency and comparable sites

Questions seek non-confidential technical studies, relevant agreements, disclosure of comparable facilities, and documented complaints/violations at similar sites.

### Monitoring and decommissioning

Questions seek ongoing reporting, independent compliance testing, complaint procedures, expected useful life, closure responsibilities, site restoration, and financial assurance.

## Evidence standard for answers

For dataset purposes, answers should later be classified as one of:

1. **Documented commitment** — written plan, permit application, engineering study, agreement, or formal company submission.
2. **Company representation** — verbal/written company statement without independent verification.
3. **Independent verification** — utility, consultant, regulator, public record, or third-party engineering result.
4. **Estimate** — preliminary number subject to design changes.
5. **Unanswered / deferred** — no responsive answer or answer postponed pending engineering/application work.

This classification will make it possible to compare what was promised at the forum with later plans and approvals without treating every statement as equally binding.

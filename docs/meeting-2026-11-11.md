## Definitions approved for inclusion in the draft

### 1. Data center — approved, exact tally unconfirmed (5–3 or 6–2)

> “Data center” means a structure or building rehabilitated or constructed to house a group of networked server computers in one physical location in order to centralize the storage, management, and dissemination of data and information pertaining to a particular business, taxonomy, or body of knowledge. A data center business’s facility typically includes the mechanical and electrical systems, redundant or backup power supplies, redundant data communications connections, environmental controls, and fire suppression systems.

**Source comparator:** Iowa Code §423.3(95)(f)(1) (2026), [official verbatim text](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/2026/423.3.pdf). The committee's wording adds “structure or” to the statute's “building” language and does not include every clause present in the state tax-program definition. **The Iowa statute is a sales-and-use-tax provision, not a general municipal zoning enactment.**

### 2. Data center business — approved, exact tally unconfirmed (5–3 or 6–2)

> “Data center business” means an entity whose business among other businesses, is to operate or lease a data center in the city of cascade.

**Source comparator:** Iowa Code §423.3(95)(f)(2) (2026), [official verbatim text](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/2026/423.3.pdf). The committee's wording adds “in the city of cascade”; capitalization, punctuation, and the rest of the reported sentence have been preserved as supplied. **“Operate or lease” does not by itself specify a land-use permit or regulatory duty.**

### 3. Sustainable design — unanimously approved (numerical vote not independently confirmed)

> “Sustainable design” Construction design that reduces negative environmental effects and improves occupant health and comfort through resource conservation, waste minimization, and healthier environments.

**Source comparator:** Iowa Code §103A.3(25) (2026), [official verbatim text](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/2026/103A.3.pdf), defines sustainable design in the state building-code context. The committee's reported sentence is a **paraphrase, not a verbatim statutory quotation**. See also Iowa Code §103A.8B, [official text](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/2026/103A.8B.pdf), regarding sustainable design and construction requirements in its own statutory context. No enforceable efficiency threshold or mandatory certification follows from this definition alone.

### 4. Hyperscale Data Center — approved 5–3 (participant-reported)

**Approved threshold wording, corrected participant report:**

- **100,000 square feet or larger**
- **Electrical demand exceeding 60 megawatts**

Both conditions must apply to a Data Center. A facility at exactly 100,000 square feet satisfies the area threshold; electrical demand must be strictly **greater than 60 MW**. This is a proposed local classification, not an existing Iowa statutory definition. The prior working transcription of “more than 100,000” and “over 60 MW annually” was incorrect; the participant has clarified the text approved at the November 11 **5–3** vote. The final City minutes remain to be checked. [See draft §165.41]({{ '/draft-ordinance/' | relative_url }}).

### Possible calendar-year clarification — new vote required

The **approved** language does not specify an annual or calendar-year measurement. If the committee wishes to measure electrical demand **during each calendar year (January 1–December 31)**, the complete measurement rule must be drafted and **voted on separately**. This is a proposed future change, **not part of the November 11 approval**. MW measures power; a demand-measurement method must be specified rather than silently substituting annual energy consumption in MWh.

## Drafting and interpretation questions for the City Attorney

1. Determine whether the terms cover server rooms accessory to other principal industrial uses, cryptocurrency mining, AI/training workloads, and containerized computing; the first sentence and the illustrative “typically includes” sentence perform different functions.
2. The independent draft now cross-references the approved **Data Center** and **Hyperscale Data Center** definitions in §165.11(B)(19). Confirm that the committee agrees with this editorial alignment; the reported definition votes do not establish approval of the separate permitted-use, parking, or performance provisions.
3. Clarify whether **Data center business** is used for classification, permitting, occupancy, ownership/leases, or something else. The phrase “in the city of cascade” may be redundant for ordinances already operating inside city limits.
4. Specify the operative sustainable-design requirement, if any, and its measurable standard; a definition alone is not a mandate.

## Generator emissions — what the EPA actually regulates

A committee participant reported roughly **15 diesel generators** associated with Simple Mining's approximately **40 MW preliminary facility**. **Neither the 15-unit inventory nor their EPA Tier rating is verified against plans**. The ratio 40/15 ≈ 2.67 MW does not prove installed engine power, Tier 4, redundancy, or emergency-only operation.

The relevant rules include federal [40 CFR Part 60, Subpart IIII](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/part-60/subpart-IIII), [40 CFR Part 63, Subpart ZZZZ](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/part-63/subpart-ZZZZ), and Iowa [567 IAC Chapter 22](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/iac/chapter/06-10-2026.567.22.pdf). The packet reproduces official, dated legal pages verbatim and highlights selected clauses.

| Limit or rule | What it actually measures |
| --- | --- |
| **15 ppm sulfur** | Applicable ultra-low-sulfur **diesel fuel**, for the under-30-L/cylinder classification under 40 CFR 60.4207(b) and 1090.305, not diesel NOx exhaust |
| **1,000 ppm sulfur** | Special federal §60.4207(d) fuel standard for engines with **at least 30 L displacement per cylinder**; check all controlling permit/fuel requirements |
| **NO₂: 100 ppb (0.100 ppm), one-hour** | EPA outdoor **ambient-air** standard and regulatory averaging form, not an exhaust-stack limit |
| **SO₂: 75 ppb (0.075 ppm), one-hour** | EPA outdoor ambient-air standard, not engine certification |
| **CO: 35 ppm (one hour) / 9 ppm (eight hours)** | EPA outdoor ambient-air standards |
| Engine NOx, CO, PM | Usually prescribed in **g/kWh**, with applicability determined by engine category, size, manufacture/installation, and emergency/nonemergency status |

**Iowa permit caution:** Iowa Chapter 22 defines emergency generator more narrowly than casual “backup” terminology and requires project-specific stationary-source permitting review. Its special 500-hour rule for some existing units is a **potential-to-emit calculation assumption**, not an authorization for 500 hours of operation. Federal maintenance/readiness testing restrictions, actual emergency operation, and prohibited peak-shaving require separate analysis.

The attached Central City **draft**, **not enacted**, has a **65 dBA site property-line limit** and routine generator testing **8 a.m.–6 p.m., Monday–Friday**, with emergency operation treated separately. Its Tier 1/2/3 size categories allow Tier 3 in M-2 instead of imposing Cascade's proposed separate Hyperscale ban. The original pages have transparent highlights without changes to the original text.

Also compared: [Palo Ordinance 93-2026](https://cityofpalo.com/?SEC=E2EC6BEE-4A87-4C36-9117-03F3AC77288B&Type=B_BASIC) (enacted EU-1 data-center district); [City of Dubuque](https://www.cityofdubuque.org/datacenters) (2026 temporary hyperscale moratorium); [Council Bluffs Council records](https://councilbluffsia.new.swagit.com/videos/400828) (existing industrial permissions and unconfirmed tier proposal); and Decorah zoning proposals, status requiring City Clerk verification. **Jones County** and **Dubuque County** draft proposals are identified separately as **county** instruments, not municipal city law.

## Questions to ask Simple Mining and Iowa DNR

1. What are the **actual number, make, model, year, mechanical and electrical ratings, cylinders and displacement** of every diesel generator?
2. What certification (Tier 2, 3 or 4) applies to **each** engine, and what are the manufacturer's EPA engine-family documents?
3. Are generators **emergency-only** or intended for demand response, peak shaving, continuous generation, or utility dispatch?
4. What are the projected **NOx, CO, SO₂, VOC, PM₂.₅, PM₁₀, HAP** emissions per engine and combined, and what is the basis for dispersion modeling at nearby receptors?
5. Has Iowa DNR received or issued any **construction permit, operating permit, PSD/Title V determination or other relevant action** for the anticipated generator fleet?
6. What are the **testing schedule, simultaneous operation limits, sound barriers, fuel specifications and spill safeguards**?

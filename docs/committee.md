# Cascade Data Center Advisory Committee

> **Status note:** The committee was created by the City Council in July 2026 and members were appointed in August. Resolution 92-26, intended to clarify voting membership, quorum, procedures, and advisory roles, was tabled at the September 14 Council meeting. The September 28 Council agenda packet published a revised proposed Resolution 92-26 with the roster below. Until final September 28 minutes or an executed resolution are available, this page labels the roster **proposed** rather than finally adopted.

## Why the proposed structure changed

The City Administrator's September 24 memo in the September 28 packet says the revised structure would use:

- two City Council representatives;
- two Planning & Zoning representatives; and
- four citizen representatives.

The memo states that the two CEDC members were removed after discussion with the City Attorney and the NDA that had been signed. Those individuals could still participate, where applicable, in non-voting or advisory capacities if separately listed.

## Committee authority

The proposed Resolution 92-26 describes the committee as **advisory only**. Its intended scope includes research and recommendations concerning:

- zoning and land use;
- water and wastewater;
- electric usage;
- cooling systems;
- noise;
- environmental issues;
- fire protection;
- construction;
- economic impacts;
- public safety;
- community safeguards; and
- proposed ordinance language.

The proposed resolution expressly states that the committee does **not** have authority to approve or deny a project, spend City funds, execute agreements, grant permits, or exercise authority reserved to the City Council or another governmental body.

## Meeting procedure in the proposed resolution

The September 28 draft also provides that:

- a Chair and Vice Chair would be selected by the voting members;
- meetings would follow Iowa Code Chapter 21;
- agendas would be posted at least 24 hours before meetings;
- meetings would be open sessions and official minutes would be maintained;
- public attendance would be allowed as required by Iowa law; and
- the meetings are intended to function as working sessions rather than debate forums.

## Cascade-specific project context (not final approvals)

Based on Simple Mining's **October 1 representations**, the present concept involves a permanent ~60,000-square-foot building on **15.22 acres**, approximately **40 MW** demand, roughly **15 backup diesel generators**, and **20-25 projected permanent jobs**. The parcel acreage is independently documented; the remaining design, staffing, operating and value figures are preliminary and subject to final plans and agreements. A concept sketch and approximate neighborhood distances do not establish measured noise or permit compliance.

## Recommended committee work product

The purpose of this independent proposal is **to review proposed neutral, measurable standards**, not to approve any particular development. A useful outcome is an agreed list of changes to transmit to the City Attorney and Planning & Zoning for review:

1. Confirm that the same impact thresholds apply to similarly situated M-2 uses.
2. Settle acoustic, generator, cooling/water, utility, setbacks, emergency, drainage and infrastructure provisions.
3. Review new implementation tools: generator-equipment documentation, verification after startup, three-year acoustic review, annual certification, independent review, road-use security and decommissioning assurance.
4. Identify provisions requiring engineering or legal review, including any vibration limit or extraterritorial jurisdiction.
5. Agree on a written next-step process and schedule for the appropriate City bodies.

**Evidence rule:** A developer's statement, a competitor's example, a sample county ordinance, a proposed City amendment, and an enacted Iowa law are not interchangeable. The current proposal is **not adopted law**, and this website does **not** speak for the City of Cascade.

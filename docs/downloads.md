# Current November 11, 2026 red/blue ordinance PDF and historical downloads

**Independent research — not adopted City law.** **[Download the updated November 11, 2026 RED/BLUE draft ordinance PDF]({{ '/downloads/cascade-m2-red-blue-draft-ordinance-2026-11-11.pdf' | relative_url }}).** The print-ready integrated redline has the four participant-reported committee-approved definitions, while identifying other independently proposed provisions as *not* approved by those votes. **Black = existing text; red = proposed ordinance text; blue = source copyedits.** This is not enacted City law.

**November 11 definition vote status:** Data Center, Data Center Business, Sustainable Design, and Hyperscale Data Center were reported approved for inclusion in the committee draft. The proposed Hyperscale threshold is **100,000 square feet or larger AND electrical demand exceeding 60 MW**. The other 24 independent definition proposals and separate ordinance clauses were **not approved** by the definition votes. The first two definitions' exact tallies remain unconfirmed against the official minutes. [Read the meeting report]({{ '/meeting-2026-11-11/' | relative_url }}).

**Historical archive:** All PDFs below dated November 3–9 predate those November 11 votes and must not be substituted for the current red/blue version.

## Original external research PDF: U.S. Chamber 2026

The original [**U.S. Chamber economic impacts report (72-page PDF)**](https://www.uschamber.com/assets/documents/The-Community-and-Economic-Impacts-of-Data-Centers.pdf) is available directly from the publisher. The [independent Iowa/Cascade evaluation]({{ '/economic-impact-chamber-2026/' | relative_url }}) and [source provenance](https://github.com/ttbakiatwoam/cascade-data-center/blob/master/sources/economic-studies/US_CHAMBER_DATA_CENTER_IMPACTS_2026_SOURCE_RECORD.md) have been added to this project. **This is an economic study, not an ordinance appendix.** It is not incorporated into the November 3 zoning/court PDFs, which remain historically dated.

**Publishing workflow:** The current November 11 PDF is generated from the November 11 source redline and published to this site's downloads with a dated file name. Previous November 3–9 PDFs remain unchanged as historical records. The November 18 combined committee packet has not been created by this PDF.

**Related legal research:** [Iowa Code §414.2 and other Iowa city/county data-center ordinances]({{ '/iowa-zoning-uniformity/' | relative_url }}) (county §335.4 and city §414.5 exceptions explained).

**Important legal distinctions:** Iowa's tax definition of a data center does not automatically control zoning. The legal-dictionary columns now use freely accessible Cornell Wex and FindLaw entries, clearly distinguishing direct definitions from related context. A preliminary injunction or negotiated settlement is not necessarily a final judicial merits ruling. The 27-case underlying status research was last compiled October 28, 2026, even though explanatory abstracts and source notes were added November 3.

## Original external research PDF: U.S. Chamber 2026

The original [**U.S. Chamber economic impacts report (72-page PDF)**](https://www.uschamber.com/assets/documents/The-Community-and-Economic-Impacts-of-Data-Centers.pdf) is available directly from the publisher. The [independent Iowa/Cascade evaluation]({{ '/economic-impact-chamber-2026/' | relative_url }}) and [source provenance](https://github.com/ttbakiatwoam/cascade-data-center/blob/master/sources/economic-studies/US_CHAMBER_DATA_CENTER_IMPACTS_2026_SOURCE_RECORD.md) have been added to this project. **This is an economic study, not an ordinance appendix.** It is not incorporated into the November 3 zoning/court PDFs, which remain historically dated.

**How historical PDF publication worked:** The earlier GitHub Actions workflow converted the then-current Markdown and zoning scan into dated PDF sources and combined packets. **Those binary files have not been regenerated from the November 11 voted-definition drafts.** The active ordinance source files and both website redlines supersede the historical ordinance PDFs; other research attachments remain archived.

**Related legal research:** [Iowa Code §414.2 and other Iowa city/county data-center ordinances]({{ '/iowa-zoning-uniformity/' | relative_url }}) (county §335.4 and city §414.5 exceptions explained).

**Important legal distinctions:** Iowa's tax definition of a data center does not automatically control zoning. The legal-dictionary columns now use freely accessible Cornell Wex and FindLaw entries, clearly distinguishing direct definitions from related context. A preliminary injunction or negotiated settlement is not necessarily a final judicial merits ruling. The 27-case underlying status research was last compiled October 28, 2026, even though explanatory abstracts and source notes were added November 3.

# How far are the proposed facilities from nearby homes?

Two images help answer this question. The **September 25, 2026 preliminary concept** places the proposed building, cooling yard, electrical yard and substation within the Simple Mining site. A **Dubuque County parcel-map screenshot** marks the land between that site and the houses east of it as **400.50 feet wide**.

The presentation also states that the main building is **216–291 feet from every property line**, is about **25 feet tall at the eave and 31 feet at the peak**, and has a proposed **tree row along the east side**. These are developer statements about a concept, not as-built measurements. The building is set back within the Simple Mining parcel, so the building-to-house distance is **more than 400.5 feet**.

**These are estimates, not certified setbacks.** The concept is preliminary, and some equipment edges are concealed by labels. The closest rooflines are approximated from aerial imagery. The estimate can change by 50–100 feet or more after a survey or plan revision.

## Why the distinction matters for noise

Distance does not establish an actual residential noise level. A continuous cooling source can sound different from a generator test, transformer hum or traffic. Low frequencies, audible tones, shielding, source height, nighttime weather and multiple units must be accounted for.

The correct next step is an independent acoustic model using **actual equipment locations and occupied home receptors**, followed by measurements after construction. Any standards the City adopts should apply equally to comparable **M-2 uses**, not just this applicant.

## Official verbatim Iowa law and EPA regulations

- **Iowa Code §455B.131:** [air contaminant, air pollution, emission, major stationary source — complete statutory text](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/2026/455B.131.pdf).
- **Iowa Code §§455B.144–.145:** [local air programs](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/2026/455B.144.pdf) and [state review](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/2026/455B.145.pdf).
- **Iowa Administrative Code 567—Ch. 22:** [air construction permits, exceptions and source registration](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/iac/chapter/567.22.pdf); **Ch. 23:** [emission standards and incorporation of federal rules](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/iac/chapter/567.23.pdf).
- **EPA:** [40 CFR Part 60 Subpart IIII](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-60/subpart-IIII) (stationary diesel NSPS), specifically [§60.4211](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-60/subpart-IIII/section-60.4211) (emergency operating limits); [40 CFR Part 63 Subpart ZZZZ](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-63/subpart-ZZZZ) (RICE air-toxics standards).
- **Iowa DNR's source guidance:** [Regulations for Data Centers](https://www.iowadnr.gov/programs-services/business-regulatory-assistance/regulations-data-centers).

**Important:** Air-construction permits and local zoning are separate regulatory programs. EPA engine certification requirements depend on engine-specific facts. The often-cited 100 annual hours for emergency-engine readiness testing and limited additional activities **does not mean 100 + 50 hours**. A proposed “Tier IV” local requirement is **not** automatically the federal legal standard for all stationary emergency diesel engines.

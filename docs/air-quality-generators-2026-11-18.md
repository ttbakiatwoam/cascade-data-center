**Next advisory meeting (participant report): Wednesday, November 18, 2026, 7:00 p.m.** The committee is scheduled to discuss Iowa and federal air regulations and stationary diesel backup generators. **Compare Iowa city ordinances first**, distinguishing hyperscale-only restrictions from ordinary standalone data-center rules; **county ordinances are deferred to a later Chapter 335 phase**. **The City agenda and any changes to meeting logistics should be verified with the City.**

[Read the complete detailed briefing, side-by-side comparison, and source record on GitHub](https://github.com/ttbakiatwoam/cascade-data-center/blob/master/dataset/policy/48_AIR_POLLUTION_GENERATORS_COMPARATIVE_BRIEF_2026-11-18.md).

## Official verbatim Iowa law and EPA regulations

- **Iowa Code §455B.131:** [air contaminant, air pollution, emission, major stationary source — complete statutory text](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/2026/455B.131.pdf).
- **Iowa Code §§455B.144–.145:** [local air programs](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/2026/455B.144.pdf) and [state review](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/2026/455B.145.pdf).
- **Iowa Administrative Code 567—Ch. 22:** [air construction permits, exceptions and source registration](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/iac/chapter/567.22.pdf); **Ch. 23:** [emission standards and incorporation of federal rules](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/iac/chapter/567.23.pdf).
- **EPA:** [40 CFR Part 60 Subpart IIII](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-60/subpart-IIII) (stationary diesel NSPS), specifically [§60.4211](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-60/subpart-IIII/section-60.4211) (emergency operating limits); [40 CFR Part 63 Subpart ZZZZ](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-63/subpart-ZZZZ) (RICE air-toxics standards).
- **Iowa DNR's source guidance:** [Regulations for Data Centers](https://www.iowadnr.gov/programs-services/business-regulatory-assistance/regulations-data-centers).

**Important:** Air-construction permits and local zoning are separate regulatory programs. EPA engine certification requirements depend on engine-specific facts. The often-cited 100 annual hours for emergency-engine readiness testing and limited additional activities **does not mean 100 + 50 hours**. A proposed “Tier IV” local requirement is **not** automatically the federal legal standard for all stationary emergency diesel engines.

## City ordinances first: hyperscale and ordinary data centers

**[Open the source-linked city-only ordinance comparison and Iowa home-rule guide]({{ '/city-data-center-ordinances/' | relative_url }})**. The comparison excludes micro/edge/ancillary server rooms as independent regulated facilities; that research-scope choice does not modify Cascade's voted Data Center definition.

| City | Ordinance / legislative status | How different scales are handled |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **City of Dubuque** | **Enacted six-month hyperscale moratorium**, September 21, 2026 | **Any one** of ≥100,000 sq ft, >75 MW, ≥5,000 servers, or >50 gpm water. Not an ordinary-center moratorium |
| **Palo** | **Enacted** EU-1 data-center district, Ord. 93-2026 | Both **large and small standalone** data centers require EU-1 siting and site plan |
| **Central City** | **November 10 draft; not enacted** | Tier 1 ≤30,000 sq ft; Tier 2 >30,000 and <100,000; Tier 3 ≥100,000, all **proposed permitted in M-2** |
| **Council Bluffs** | Existing I-2/P-I permissions; later tiers **discussed, unverified enacted** | Draft council discussion of ≤30 MW versus >30 MW |
| **Decorah** | M-3 proposal advanced in 2026; **final enactment unverified** | Data Center as principal M-3 use; no verified hyperscale-only threshold |
| **Cascade independent proposal** | Not enacted; **5–3 definition vote** is not a ban vote | **Both** ≥100,000 sq ft AND demand >60 MW for Hyperscale; new citywide prohibition separately proposed |

**Separate legal authority:** City zoning is principally [Iowa Code Chapter 414](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/2026/414.pdf). County zoning is governed by [Chapter 335](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/2026/335.pdf); it usually covers **unincorporated land**. Municipal home rule §364.1 does not displace zoning procedures, and §414.23 does not automatically extend Cascade's authority two miles. See [the detailed jurisdiction analysis]({{ '/city-data-center-ordinances/' | relative_url }}).

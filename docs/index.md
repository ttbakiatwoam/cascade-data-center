# Cascade Data Center Research & Zoning Work

> **Independent community research - not an official City of Cascade website.**  
> The working position of this project is pro-development with clear, measurable community protections, transparency, and accountability.

## Current committee work

The Data Center Advisory Committee is expected to continue meeting. The **next proposed meeting date is October 19, 2026**.

The immediate goal is to move from repeated issue-identification to review of concrete zoning language that can be revised by the Advisory Committee, Planning & Zoning, City staff, the City Attorney, and ultimately the City Council.

## Core policy principle

Any zoning update should apply **uniformly to similarly situated M-2 businesses**. Data centers can be defined as a use type, but noise, lighting, screening, water, major electrical demand, generators, emergency planning, infrastructure, stormwater, and decommissioning standards should be district-wide or triggered by neutral impact thresholds.

## Important status note

The proposal and ordinance on this site are working documents for discussion. They are not adopted City policy, an approved development application, legal advice, engineering approval, or a representation that the proposed Simple Mining project has been approved or denied.

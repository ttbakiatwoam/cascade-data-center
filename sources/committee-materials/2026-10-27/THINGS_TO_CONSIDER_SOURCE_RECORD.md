## Complete substantive text

**OTHER THINGS TO CONSIDER FOR DATA CENTER ORDINANCE**

- Emissions from 15 generators
- Noise from 15 generators
- Regulations for a data center potential within 2 miles of Cascade (larger scale data center)

## Treatment in the repository

1. **Generator emissions:** Already considered in the research and the original October 6 M-2 draft, but final equipment models, aggregate rated power, fuel, air-permit determinations, testing schedule, and modeled emissions have not been independently established. The October 26 proposed revision requests a generator equipment schedule and applicable air-permit documentation, while preserving state/federal emissions jurisdiction.
2. **Generator noise:** The October 6 draft has dBA/dBC limits, a tonal adjustment, planned test hours, modeling, and operating records. The October 26 proposal adds explicit simultaneous-operation acoustic scenarios and strengthens independent verification.
3. **A potential facility within two miles:** A city M-2 ordinance does not automatically govern unincorporated sites. Iowa Code §414.23 allows extended city zoning jurisdiction only in qualifying areas **without county zoning**. Iowa Code §354.9 provides a separate, ordinance-based review power over certain subdivision plats. Any potential site must be identified by parcel and county before determining the responsible authority.

These points are discussed in [October 26 packet review](../../../dataset/updates/37_ADVISORY_PACKET_REVIEW_2026-10-26.md), [local zoning authority](../../../dataset/policy/04_CASCADE_ZONING_AND_LOCAL_AUTHORITY.md), and the [October 26 proposed M-2 revision](../../../proposals/DRAFT-ORDINANCE-M2-DATA-CENTER-PERFORMANCE-STANDARDS-2026-10-26.md).

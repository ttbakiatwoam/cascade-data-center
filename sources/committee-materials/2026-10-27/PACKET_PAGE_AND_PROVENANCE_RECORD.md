## Particular clauses warranting review

**Greene County, pp. 9–12:** Facility-area limits, setbacks, noise, landscape planning, utility and water information, stormwater, access, emergency response, liability insurance, decommissioning, and ongoing operator responsibilities.

**Decorah proposal, pp. 13–16:** Definition and zoning treatment of data centers, utility service analysis, closed-loop cooling, setbacks, site/noise standards, fire and emergency plans, generator backup-power plans, screening and lighting, and decommissioning.

**Jackson County working draft, pp. 30–56:**
- pp. 35–37: Application completeness, utility service coordination, applicant-funded independent review, and annual compliance certification;
- pp. 37–39: Distances from occupied buildings and property lines; **do not copy** these numeric setbacks into Cascade without a buildability and legal review;
- pp. 39–41: Noise and *separate physical vibration* measurements; post-startup and recurring tests. The draft proposes 0.30 mm/s daytime and 0.14 mm/s nighttime peak particle velocity. These are proposal values from Jackson County, **not adopted or validated standards for Cascade**;
- pp. 41–44: Cooling, containment, water-use reports, utility verification, emergency response and safety coordination;
- pp. 48–50: Preconstruction road-condition surveys, road-use agreements, repair/security requirements. The draft discusses financial assurance at 130% of estimated road costs; it is **not** a Cascade standard;
- pp. 50–53: Decommissioning plan, review/update of cost estimates, financial assurance, owner/operator responsibility and transfer provisions;
- pp. 53–56: Enforcement, continuing violations, periodic ordinance review and jurisdiction/conflict notes.

**Cascade original draft, pp. 58–72:** Original October 6 text is historically preserved. A separately dated October 26 working revision proposes narrower, reviewable enforcement changes and keeps the city/county jurisdiction distinction explicit.

**Simple Mining slides, pp. 73–90:** Presentation confirms the *represented* move to a permanent building, a ~15.2-acre location, ~40 MW power, recirculating coolant, stated noise goals, backup generators, planned staffing and local economic commitments. No final equipment schedule, permits, acoustic model or binding agreement appears in this packet.

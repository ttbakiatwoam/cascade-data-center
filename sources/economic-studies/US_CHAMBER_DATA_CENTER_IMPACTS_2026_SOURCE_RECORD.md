## Classification

Original institutional economic modeling and economic advocacy. **Primary evidence of what the U.S. Chamber published, not primary evidence of what Cascade will collect or Simple Mining will build.** The source models scenarios using REMI PI+, rather than measuring an operating Cascade site. The 50 MW site is a **comparator**, not the approximately 40 MW proposal.

## Material findings and limits

- Iowa (p. 32): **4,947 statewide jobs supported annually during the two-year construction phase**; **647 statewide jobs supported annually in the operating phase**. Iowa annual GDP impacts: **$592.470m** in construction and **$138.947m** in operation. They include **indirect and induced** employment and are **not on-site Cascade job counts**.
- The model explicitly assumes **50 direct data-center employees** (p. 68), plus five electric and five water utility workers, and **$1.69 billion** of two-year build spending; $1 billion in equipment refreshes every five years is also assumed. These are not Cascade public commitments.
- Iowa wage table (pp. 9, 11): annual earnings **$117,260** in industry NAICS 518210 versus **$67,340** across all industries; includes bonuses/other compensation and no adjustment for occupation.
- The four county fiscal totals are **gross**, before abatements/exemptions, and none evaluates Cascade or an Iowa county.
- Report discussion of power rates, noise and cooling provides **no measured Cascade noise data, metered water requirements, signed MVEC service contract, or project-specific rate study**.

## Municipal versus county power — cite before adopting any prohibition

- **City municipal home rule:** Iowa Constitution art. III, §38A, Iowa Code **§364.1**; **municipal zoning is exercised under Chapter 414** (§§414.1–.4), as emphasized by Iowa Attorney General Opinion 05-2-1 (2005). City home rule is not a substitute for zoning procedures.
- **County home rule and county zoning:** Iowa Constitution art. III, §39A; Code **§331.301** and **§335.3/.4**, generally for **unincorporated land**. A county data-center use category cannot be silently incorporated as municipal authority.
- **Jurisdiction outside Cascade:** Iowa Code **§414.23** permits only a **conditional** extension up to two miles by ordinance, except where the affected county already has zoning; additional commission/board requirements apply. Do not say Cascade controls a blanket two-mile “permit zone.”
- **Separate approval needed:** November 11's **5–3 Hyperscale definition vote** is not approval of proposed **§165.12(17)** citywide prohibition. Any annual/calendar-year demand method must also be separately voted on.

## Proposed citywide prohibition - separate policy vote and jurisdiction review

**Proposed, not yet approved:** The November 11 committee voted **5–3 on the definition** of Hyperscale Data Center, with *at least 100,000 square feet* **and** *electrical demand exceeding 60 MW*. This November 18 packet proposes a **different operative policy**: no Hyperscale Data Center may be permitted in any district subject to Cascade's lawful zoning authority. The proposal uses §165.12(17) to avoid unintentionally granting a special exception through §165.11(D)(8). It must be considered and voted on separately from the definition. The City Clerk and attorney should verify current §165.12 numbering, proper amendment procedure, zoning uniformity, treatment of existing lawful uses, and where Iowa Code §414.23 applies outside municipal limits.

## Hyperscale measurement question for November 18 — optional new vote

**Approved November 11 (5–3):** “100,000 square feet or larger” **and** “Electrical demand exceeding 60 megawatts.” The earlier “more than 100,000” and “annually” wording was a project transcription error corrected by the committee participant. **Do not alter the approved text without a new vote.**

**Clarification the committee may consider:** Should the applicable demand threshold be assessed using a maximum coincident demand, an agreed utility-record demand metric, or another documented MW measurement? If the committee wishes to evaluate demand **by calendar year (January 1 through December 31)**, that specific time basis and underlying method must be proposed and **approved through a separate vote**; it was not approved November 11. Distinguish MW power demand from MWh electrical energy use.

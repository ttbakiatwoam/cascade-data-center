## Hyperscale measurement question for November 18 — optional new vote

**Approved November 11 (5–3):** “100,000 square feet or larger” **and** “Electrical demand exceeding 60 megawatts.” The earlier “more than 100,000” and “annually” wording was a project transcription error corrected by the committee participant. **Do not alter the approved text without a new vote.**

**Clarification the committee may consider:** Should the applicable demand threshold be assessed using a maximum coincident demand, an agreed utility-record demand metric, or another documented MW measurement? If the committee wishes to evaluate demand **by calendar year (January 1 through December 31)**, that specific time basis and underlying method must be proposed and **approved through a separate vote**; it was not approved November 11. Distinguish MW power demand from MWh electrical energy use.

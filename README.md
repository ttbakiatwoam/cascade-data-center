# Cascade Data Center

Research, source material, community questions, and policy analysis related to proposed data-center development in Cascade, Iowa.

The working position of this independent community project is **pro-data-center development with appropriate community protections, transparency, and accountability**. Read the [research scope and project stance](docs/research-scope.md) for the full purpose, areas of focus, and policy principles.

## How to read the evidence

Public records, company/utility representations, secondary reporting, and project-generated analysis have different evidentiary weight. Preliminary figures and draft policies retain their labels. See the [methodology and privacy rules](dataset/00_INDEX.md).

**The October 1 transcript was created by YouTube using its automated closed-captioning system.** The [raw WebVTT file](sources/community-forums/2026-10-01_simple-mining-community-forum_youtube-auto-captions.en.vtt) is preserved unchanged and is not a human-verified or certified verbatim transcript.

Technology-context research does not establish which workloads the Cascade facility will run or which broader benefits it will deliver. The public repository excludes private correspondence, private social-media discussions, personal disputes, and personal contact information.

## Email to the Mayor and City Council

Prepared October 6, 2026 to accompany the [draft M-2 data center and performance standards amendment](proposals/DRAFT-ORDINANCE-M2-DATA-CENTER-PERFORMANCE-STANDARDS-2026-10-06.md). Published here at the author's request. This is the prepared email text; delivery is not established by this repository. The Word and PDF attachments mentioned in the email were prepared separately and are not currently stored here; the linked Markdown draft is the version available in this repository.

<details>
<summary>Read the email: Draft M-2 Data Center Zoning Amendment - Time to Move This Forward</summary>

**Subject: Draft M-2 Data Center Zoning Amendment - Time to Move This Forward**

Mayor Knepper and Members of the City Council,

At this point, I think it is time to actually get the ball rolling on the zoning work. We have spent months discussing the same issues, holding meetings, gathering questions, reviewing neighboring ordinances, and hearing from the developer. Enough delay. We now have enough information to put actual ordinance language on the table and begin working from a concrete document instead of continuing to discuss the concept in the abstract.

I have taken the liberty of preparing a **draft amendment to Cascade's M-2 Heavy Industrial zoning regulations**. I have attached the draft in Word and PDF format, along with the Markdown source.

The draft uses three primary sources of information:

- the comments, questions, answers, and representations contained in the transcript of the October 1 Simple Mining community meeting;
- the data-center ordinances and draft ordinances adopted or considered by neighboring Iowa jurisdictions, particularly Linn County, Jones County, Jackson County, and other regional comparators; and
- established industry and technical standards for issues such as noise, cooling, emergency planning, electrical infrastructure, backup generation, lighting, screening, water use, and decommissioning.

Importantly, I did **not** write this as a "Simple Mining ordinance."

The draft adds **Data Center / High-Density Computing Facility** as an expressly recognized use within the M-2 Heavy Industrial District, but the substantive performance standards are written to apply **uniformly to all similarly situated M-2 businesses**.

For example, if Cascade is going to establish rules concerning industrial noise, generator testing, exterior lighting, large electrical loads, water consumption, emergency-response planning, or major industrial development, those rules should apply to any business creating those impacts - not merely to a data center because that happens to be the project currently in front of us.

The draft therefore uses objective thresholds such as electrical demand, water consumption, stationary generation capacity, and energy-storage capacity to define a **Major Industrial Facility** requiring additional review. A future factory, manufacturing plant, large refrigeration facility, or other industrial user crossing the same thresholds would be subject to the same standards.

I believe that is a much more defensible and useful way to modernize Cascade's zoning ordinance.

The draft includes provisions addressing:

- M-2 data centers as a defined permitted use;
- Major Industrial Facility review;
- measurable property-line and residential noise standards;
- low-frequency and tonal noise;
- preconstruction acoustic modeling and post-construction verification;
- generator testing hours and operating records;
- setbacks for major exterior mechanical equipment;
- lighting and screening;
- large water users and cooling systems;
- utility verification for major electrical loads;
- fuel, batteries, hazardous materials, and emergency-response planning;
- stormwater and drainage;
- infrastructure impacts;
- decommissioning and financial assurance; and
- material expansion of an approved facility.

I have also clearly marked the document as a **draft for discussion and legal review, not adopted law**. It should obviously be reviewed by the City Attorney, Planning and Zoning, City staff, engineers where necessary, and ultimately the Council before anything is introduced or adopted.

But someone has to put a first draft on the table.

That is what I am doing here.

The October 1 meeting answered enough of the major factual questions that we should now be able to stop asking what an ordinance *might* contain and begin debating the actual numbers, thresholds, standards, and language we want Cascade to adopt.

I would have included the Planning and Zoning Commission on this email as well, but I have not been able to locate a public email address for the Commission. Please feel free to forward this draft to them and include it in any appropriate Planning and Zoning materials.

I am not suggesting that every word or number in this draft is perfect. I am suggesting that it is time for the City to start editing something.

Please consider this a working document intended to move that process forward.

Thank you,

**Andrew Saunders**\
Cascade, Iowa

</details>

## Maintaining and navigating the collection

Use [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) when adding evidence. The [repository layout and file move map](docs/repository-layout.md) explain where existing files went. Original numbered note names are retained, and the [research index](dataset/00_INDEX.md) remains the central entry point.

This is not an official repository of the City of Cascade, Simple Mining, or any utility. Nothing here is legal, engineering, environmental, financial, or professional advice. Review the source documents directly when making policy or regulatory decisions.
